Frequently asked questions

Is ManaPal free?

Yes. ManaPal is free to download, and the collection, prices, scanner, sets, precons and life counter are free forever, with up to 7 built decks. AI actions run on Mana: one quick AI question is free every day and your first Find your Commander is free. ManaPal Pro is optional and adds Mana every month, advanced stats and unlimited decks, binders and playtest. Learn more

Can ManaPal scan Magic: The Gathering cards?

Yes. Point your iPhone camera at a card and ManaPal recognizes it, shows its price and adds it to your collection. You can pick the set, mark it as foil and keep scanning without leaving the camera. Learn more

How do I find out how much my MTG collection is worth?

Add your cards to ManaPal by scanning them or importing a list: the collection screen shows the total value, updated with current market prices, and how it changes over time. Learn more

Can ManaPal build a Commander deck from the cards I own?

Yes. With "Build me a deck with my cards", Mote builds a 100-card Commander deck using the cards in your collection first, and lists the few you are missing. Learn more

How do I choose the right commander?

Start from how you like to win, your favorite colors and your budget. In ManaPal, the "Find the right Commander for you" quiz asks 12 questions and suggests commanders in budget, mid and top lists. Learn more

How many lands should a Commander deck run?

Most Commander decks play 36 to 38 lands plus about 10 ramp pieces. Go lower with a cheap curve and lots of ramp, higher with many expensive spells or landfall payoffs. ManaPal shows your land count, mana curve and opening-hand odds. Learn more

Does ManaPal have a life counter with commander damage?

Yes. The life counter handles 2 to 6 players with commander damage, poison, monarch, initiative, Planechase and Archenemy, and saves every game in your stats. Learn more

Can I import my collection from ManaBox or Moxfield?

Yes. ManaPal imports collections from ManaBox, Moxfield, Archidekt, Delver Lens, Dragon Shield, Deckbox, TCGplayer and Cardmarket CSV files, and decks from Archidekt, Moxfield, ManaBox, MTG Arena or a plain text list. Learn more

Can ManaPal answer Magic rules questions?

Yes. The official Comprehensive Rules and every card's official rulings are in the app and work offline, and you can look up several cards at once to see how they interact. For a tricky interaction, Ask Mote reads the rules and the rulings and gives you a verdict with the rules it relied on. At a tournament, the judge still has the final say.

Can I play with my friends in ManaPal?

Yes. Create a free online profile, add friends with a QR code, a link or a friend code, and the games you play together show up on everyone's phone, with stats for your playgroup. Learn more

Where are my cards and decks stored?

On your device and in your own iCloud, synced across your iPhone and iPad. We can't see them and we don't sell them. Learn more

Is ManaPal an official Wizards of the Coast app?

No. ManaPal is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. It is not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast. Learn more
