ManaPal Support

Something not working, an idea, or a card that shows up wrong? We read every message.

Write to us: albosapps@gmail.com
Tell us your device, iOS version and ManaPal version, and add a screenshot if you can: it helps us fix things faster.

Common questions

My collection doesn't appear on my other device

Collection and decks sync through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID, that iCloud is turned on for ManaPal in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, and that both have an internet connection. The first sync of a large collection can take a few minutes.

I bought Mana or Pro but don't see it

Make sure you're using the same Apple ID you bought with and that you're signed in with Apple in ManaPal, then reopen the app. If it still doesn't appear, use the restore purchases option in the app or write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I cancel ManaPal Pro?

On iPhone or iPad go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose ManaPal and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal. You keep Pro until the period ends.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and choose the purchase.

An AI action failed. Did I lose my Mana?

No. If an AI action fails, the Mana goes back to your balance automatically. If you think something went wrong, write to us.

A card was recognized wrong or a price looks off

Change the printing with the Set button or search for the card by name. If a card or price is consistently wrong, send us the card name and set so we can look into it.

How do I add a friend?

In the Games tab, create your profile, then tap Add friend in Your playgroup: show your QR code, send your invite link or type your friend's code. When you play a game with a linked friend, it shows up on their phone too.

Can I hide a shared game, or block someone?

Yes. Each player can hide a shared game from their own history. From a friend's page you can remove them, block them or report them: a report is an email to us, and we look at every one.

How do I turn off notifications?

ManaPal notifies you when a friend adds you or shares a game. To stop them, go to Settings › Notifications › ManaPal on your iPhone or iPad.

How do I delete my data?

Data in your iCloud can be deleted from Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › ManaPal. To delete your online profile, friendships and the games you shared, open your profile and tap Delete profile. To delete everything linked to your account on our server (Mana balance, Pro status, invite codes, AI action records and your online profile), use Delete account in the account menu of the Mana store, or write to us. To stop usage statistics, turn off "Help us improve ManaPal" in Settings › Privacy. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
